# Customer Data Analysis | Power BI

### Analyzing Customer Shopping Patterns and Revenue Trends

## Project Description

This project is about analyzing customer shopping data from 10 shopping malls in Istanbul, Turkey, covering the period from 2021 to 2023.

I used Power BI to explore customer purchasing patterns based on gender, age, product categories, payment methods and shopping mall locations. The dashboard presents the analysis through different charts and visualizations to make the results easier to understand.

## Project Purpose

The main purpose of this project was to understand customer shopping behaviour, compare purchasing patterns and identify revenue trends. It also involved drawing insights from the analysis and providing suggestions that could help businesses make better decisions.

## Tech Stack

- **Power BI Desktop** – Dashboard creation and data visualization.
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation.

## Data Source

The project uses a customer shopping dataset containing transactions from 10 shopping malls in Istanbul, Turkey.

**Time Period:** 2021–2023

**Data includes:**
- Invoice Number
- Customer ID
- Gender
- Age
- Product Category
- Quantity
- Price
- Payment Method
- Invoice Date
- Shopping Mall

The dashboard displays 1,000 transactions.

## Dashboard Features & Highlights

- Gender-wise customer distribution.
- Revenue comparison between male and female customers.
- Shopping patterns across different age groups.
- Revenue analysis by age category.
- Payment method distribution.
- Payment method comparison across product categories.
- Revenue comparison across shopping malls.
- Product category analysis by customer gender.

## Key Questions

The analysis focuses on answering the following questions:

1. How are purchases distributed between male and female customers?
2. Which gender accounts for more purchases?
3. Which gender generates more revenue?
4. How do product categories vary across customers?
5. Which age groups make more purchases?
6. Which age group generates the highest revenue?
7. What are the popular product categories?
8. Which payment methods are commonly used?
9. How are payment methods related to product categories?
10. How does revenue vary across shopping malls?

## Key Insights

- Female customers account for 62% of transactions, while male customers account for 38%.
- Female customers generated approximately 1.41M in revenue, compared with approximately 0.96M from male customers.
- The 55–64 age group recorded the highest revenue, at approximately 0.47M.
- Cash was the most commonly used payment method, accounting for 42% of transactions. Credit cards accounted for 36.6%, and debit cards accounted for 21.4%.
- Revenue was compared across all 10 shopping malls.

## Business Recommendations

- Consider loyalty and referral programs to encourage repeat purchases.
- Study customer preferences across different age groups when planning promotions.
- Review product category performance to support inventory planning.
- Consider customer payment preferences when planning promotional offers.
- Compare shopping mall performance to identify areas that may need improvement.
